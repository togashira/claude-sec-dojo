# Meeting Minutes Prompt

## When to use it

You took messy notes during a meeting and want them turned into minutes, decisions, and a to-do list.

## Prompt

```
Below are raw notes I took during a meeting. Organize them into:
1. A meeting summary (3 lines or fewer)
2. Decisions made (bullet list)
3. To-dos (include owner and deadline if known; otherwise write "owner not assigned")
4. Open items / carried over for later
Do not fill gaps with guesses - if something isn't in the notes, write "not mentioned".

# Meeting info
- Date: [e.g. 2026-11-07]
- Attendees: [e.g. Jane, Mike, Partner Co.]
- Topic: [e.g. pricing strategy for the new product]

# Raw notes
[Paste your raw meeting notes here]
```

## Usage tips

- It's fine if the notes are informal or fragmented - paste them as-is
- Follow up with the relevant people on anything marked "not mentioned"
