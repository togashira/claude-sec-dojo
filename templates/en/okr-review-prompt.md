# OKR / KPI Review Prompt

## When to use it

At a quarterly or monthly checkpoint, you want to review OKR/KPI progress and think through next steps.

## Prompt

```
You are an advisor supporting a business review.
Looking at the OKR/KPI progress below, produce:
1. A summary of what went well and what's behind
2. Possible reasons for anything behind target (clearly marked as speculation)
3. 3-5 concrete action ideas for the next period
If data is missing, don't guess - list it under "information to confirm" instead.

# This period's OKR/KPI
Objective: [e.g. strengthen new customer acquisition]
- KR1: [e.g. new contracts: target 50 -> actual 32]
- KR2: [e.g. demo-to-opportunity rate: target 20% -> actual 15%]
- KR3: [e.g. ...]

# Additional context (optional)
[e.g. a new sales rep joined mid-period, a competitor ran a promotion, etc.]
```

## Usage tips

- Incomplete numbers are fine - enter what you know
- Always validate the "action ideas" with people who know the day-to-day situation before acting on them
