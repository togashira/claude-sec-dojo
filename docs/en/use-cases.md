# Use Cases

A tour of where Claude fits into management work, organized by situation, with links to matching prompt templates where one exists.

## Decision-making

- Lay out the pros and cons of several options → [decision-support-prompt.md](../../templates/en/decision-support-prompt.md)
- Surface the risks behind a pending decision
- Compare a decision against similar past ones

## Meetings & communication

- Turn raw meeting notes into minutes, decisions, and action items → [meeting-minutes-prompt.md](../../templates/en/meeting-minutes-prompt.md)
- Draft internal announcements or notices
- Rephrase a difficult message for a specific audience

## Reporting

- Turn bullet-point activity notes into a proper status report → [weekly-report-prompt.md](../../templates/en/weekly-report-prompt.md)
- Roll up reports from multiple teams into one summary

## Strategy & review

- Review OKR/KPI progress and think through next actions → [okr-review-prompt.md](../../templates/en/okr-review-prompt.md)
- Organize notes on competitors or market trends
- Get feedback on a draft business plan

## Automation (requires a tool)

- Auto-generate a recurring report in a fixed format → [tools/weekly-report-agent/](../../tools/weekly-report-agent/)
